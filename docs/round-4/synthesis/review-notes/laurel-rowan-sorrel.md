## Main recommendation

Adopt **proof-carrying residual interfaces** as the next concrete experiment, while keeping three different products distinct: a diagnostic explanation, a sound discovered conditional route, and a complete family of inclusion-minimal routes within a declared finite rule profile. Laurel and Sorrel supply the latter two semantics; Rowan supplies a simpler closure engine and a diagnostic frontier. Their common word “frontier” must not conceal this difference.

The three most valuable distinct additions are Laurel's recovery of a conditional proof after a premise disappears and its realized-observation span criterion; Rowan's executable bounded grounding, strict request sealing, and exact affine decision over supplied semilinear images; and Sorrel's residual-projection theorem, method-aware minimization, and finite-measure Cauchy–CDF transfer case. These are complementary implementation and mathematical components, not three fully implemented languages.

## Laurel

**Residualization and alternatives.** In “A calculus of residual evidence,” the proposed judgment retains a fixed goal G and a conditional proof `Γ ⊢ π : Π Δ, G`; unfinished evidence becomes an explicit telescope Δ. Publishing this as a conditional theorem changes the published statement explicitly. Meaning-bearing data are selected before a ground support engine runs; a dependent structure with an unfinished field is a partial constructor, not yet a completed object (`docs/round-4/ideas/Laurel/Laurel.tex:288`, `:298`, `:307`). This is a useful boundary for proof-implicit syntax: synthesizing a proof of a fixed property differs from inventing a witness, structure, or quantifier order.

For finite atoms V, known facts K, authorized offers O, and Horn rules R, Laurel computes

`F(q) = Min⊆ { H ⊆ O : q ∈ Cl_R(K ∪ H) }`.

Alternative routes add antichains; conjunctive premises take unions of supports and remove supersets. The soundness, termination, and relative completeness arguments are correct for the finite ground calculus. Each retained support has a separately checked conditional proof. Inclusion-minimal supports are not globally weakest mathematical assumptions, and partial saturation does not guarantee global minimality (`Laurel.tex:323`, `:348`, `:379`, `:392`; `prototype/laurel_core.py:80`, `:127`). The explicit width experiment is valuable: k independent binary choices produce 2^k incomparable supports. Bounded rule generation alone cannot prevent exponential explanation output.

**Lost-premise repair.** Laurel proposes abstracting a used context leaf `h : P` out of an existing proof to obtain `P → G`, preserving that route and omitting facts its proof never used. This can give a sufficient repair immediately, without recomputing every alternative. It is not a minimality result or general dependent-context migration (`Laurel.tex:425`). If the target or later declarations depend on the proof being removed, ordinary ground abstraction is insufficient; typed telescope rebuilding must expose that dependency. This should be a distinct operation from global alternative-route search: preserving the author's route and minimizing missing assumptions can be competing goals.

**Realized affine observations.** For admissible inputs `x : A_P` and observations `o(x) ∈ Q^k`, let `W = span_Q{(1,o(x))}`. If actual admissible witnesses `x₁,…,x_m` span every lifted admissible observation, then two affine expressions agree on every admissible input exactly when they agree on these witnesses. The proof is linear algebra on their affine difference. A finite basis can be selected from the spanning family, with `m ≤ k+1`; existence of that basis is not an effective algorithm for an arbitrary admissible type (`Laurel.tex:468`, `:483`, `:506`). Empty domains and correlated coordinates are handled correctly. Observing that sampled rank has stopped increasing does not establish the required coverage theorem. This gives a stronger formulation than testing arbitrary abstract coefficient vectors: a failing test already includes an actual source input.

**Corpus-specific lessons.** The Frey `a₄` coefficient requires evenness of b and `p ≥ 4`, while the selected `a₂` route additionally uses oddness and a residue hypothesis. Source exact divisibility, source quotient balance, and invertibility of the mapped denominator are separate stages (`Laurel.tex:554`). The sharp-Lipschitz discussion correctly distinguishes a bound from leastness; attaining the derivative bound is sufficient for sharpness, not necessary (`:624`, `:639`). Almost-everywhere transfer is tied to its measure and consumer, with explicit caution about exchanging uncountable quantifiers (`:666`).

**What is implemented.** A finite propositional parser, support engine, independent proof-tree checker, and conditional Lean exporter run. Grounding Lean expressions, validating binder roles, typed rewrite transport, dependent residuals, and a mathematical frontend remain proposals. The generated arithmetic-named propositions and rules are theorem parameters, not proved arithmetic (`Laurel.tex:768`; `README.md:52`). Of the four shipped Lean files, `cycle.lean` contains no theorem. None needs an external mathematics import; root should count declarations, not filenames.

## Rowan

**A deliberately stricter frozen request.** Rowan requires the target, universe parameters, and types/values of the local telescope to contain no unresolved metavariables before dispatch. Fixed free variables remain legitimate. Providers work in a private proof island; candidate proof holes and witness construction for a fixed existential goal are permitted without allowing the provider to rewrite the request (`docs/round-4/ideas/Rowan/Rowan.tex:560`, `:568`, `:600`). This differs from Laurel/Sorrel's proposed role classification of unresolved holes. Strict sealing is an attractive first engineering policy because its contract is easier to state, but real Lean cases should test whether it rejects legitimate elaboration patterns unnecessarily.

Rowan also identifies a serious support-authority boundary: allowing a data bundle such as `FreyPackage` may transitively expose all of its proof fields. Filtering hypothesis names alone does not establish a mathematical premise restriction. A real implementation needs a restricted typed telescope and checked declaration dependencies (`Rowan.tex:649`). The Python model's `allowed_support` filters symbolic names; it does not solve that transitive Lean dependency problem (`rowan_model.py:154`, `:179`). This is an acknowledged implementation boundary, not a newly discovered false acceptance claim.

**Executable finite grounding.** The term bank is fixed per stage. Schema data arguments come from its sorted eligible terms; fresh constructed terms require another explicit bounded stage. The upper bound on attempted assignments is a sum of products of eligible binder-pool sizes, with dependent binders checked left to right and universe choices included (`Rowan.tex:673`, `:700`, `:711`; `rowan_model.py:122`). Failed instances must count against the budget too. This is more implemented than Laurel/Sorrel's Lean-grounding proposals, though Rowan's sorts and atoms remain symbolic.

Backward exploration retains all producer alternatives in the relevant cone; a counter-based forward closure derives each atom once. Its linear bound after grounding is for a single proof per reachable atom, not the enumeration of every minimal support. The written closure soundness, completeness, termination, and rule-order invariance claims fit that model (`Rowan.tex:724`, `:770`).

**Important frontier qualification, reproduced.** “The frontier is explanatory, not a weakest-precondition oracle” says it reports unresolved leaves and falls back to the target for a leafless unseeded cycle (`Rowan.tex:779`; `rowan_model.py:254`). With rules `A → A` and `A ∧ B → Goal`, no assumptions, the model reports only B. Supplying all of this reported frontier still leaves Goal open, because the A cycle is unseeded. It then falls back to Goal. The code's “valid, nonminimal explanatory frontier” should be read as a diagnostic claim, not a sufficient residual contract. Derived-proof checking remains intact. A real interface should either mark such output diagnostic-only or carry a conditional proof for every displayed sufficient repair. Merely calling the output nonminimal does not communicate this incompleteness.

**Exact affine checking on supplied semilinear images.** For

`S = ⋃j { b_j + Σk z_k v_jk : z_k ∈ N }`,

an affine difference `D(n)=δ+α·n` over integers vanishes on S iff every `D(b_j)=0` and every `α·v_jk=0`. A failing base produces `b_j`; a failing generator with a valid base produces `b_j+v_jk`. This provides a finite concrete abstract witness without a general membership solver (`Rowan.tex:1248`, `:1278`, `:1304`). Empty unions, shifts, correlated coordinates, and redundant generators cause no mathematical difficulty. A source-domain cover is enough for positive soundness; a realizer is needed to turn a failing abstract witness into a source counterexample. Exact source completeness needs both (`:1108`, `:1320`). Inferring the exact image of arbitrary list programs, piecewise/nonlinear semantics, and proving Lean reification are outside the implementation.

The autonomous-ODE reconstruction is another useful analysis workload: recurrence depends on the derivative of the outer function only on the actual image of the trajectory, and neighborhood equality justifies successive local derivative transport (`Rowan.tex:905`). It should complement, not replace, Sorrel's measure-sensitive Cauchy case.

**Implementation and evidence.** Rowan has no shipped Lean source. Its frozen Python dataclasses are a reference-model discipline, not a security boundary against arbitrary hostile Python code; the report says this (`Rowan.tex:1800`). Its semilinear and list computations validate supplied finite models, not a source-to-model theorem. The exact quotient use site comes first, local analysis second, a Lean behavioral DSL later (`:1902`).

## Sorrel

**Arguments in progress and availability projection.** Sorrel's plan is a typed conditional proof over an explicit residual telescope. Meaning-bearing structures, quantifiers, and relations are protected; helper extraction must check the restricted context, including dependencies (`docs/round-4/ideas/Sorrel/sorrel.tex:574`, `:637`, `:657`). Its finite ground calculus designates all authorized slots H in advance, computes minimal sufficient supports L(G), and treats currently available slots `K ⊆ H` separately. The residual family is

`F_K(G) = Min⊆ { S \ K : S ∈ L(G) }`.

For every additional `U ⊆ H \ K`, G follows from K∪U exactly when U contains some member of this residual family. This theorem is correct for the finite fixed Horn profile and gives a concrete benefit: changing availability can recompute the display without redoing saturation, provided atoms, authorized slots, rules, and method policy remain unchanged (`sorrel.tex:700`, `:757`, `:817`). Laurel's formulation is recovered by treating available K as known facts and offers as H\K. These are not conflicting notions of minimality, but their cached state differs.

**Method policy before minimization.** Sorrel clearly separates the semantic Lean proof from an intensional plan certificate enforcing an allowed method grammar. It distinguishes arbitrary proof-producing leaves from restricted registered leaves. Rules must be filtered before support minimization, or labels indexed by policy; otherwise an impermissible smaller support can erase a permitted larger alternative (`sorrel.tex:676`, `:844`, `:851`). This is a correctness condition for the advertised method feature, not merely a ranking preference. A support set alone does not identify which method established it.

**Two bounded/export qualifications.** First, line845's statement that a bounded run retains a “subset of the final label families” is too strong literally: a discovered support can be dominated by a later route and disappear from the final antichain. The proof remains valid; the globally minimal inventory is not known until saturation. Laurel explicitly makes the correct distinction at `Laurel.tex:392`. The retained probe exhibits `{B}` early and `{∅}` at completion.

Second, the CLI chooses the first support ordered by total cardinality, not an available route (`sorrel_model.py:409`). For supports `{A}` and `{B,C}`, with B and C available, the status is `supported_in_model` and residual frontier `{∅}`, yet it exports the conditional route requiring A. The theorem is still a valid conditional implication. The gap is that selected-route identity is disconnected from the availability-based result. Preserve a proof per displayed residual and select a supported route first, or expose explicit route selection and its residual.

**Cauchy–CDF transfer, with useful invalid neighbors.** For a finite positive Borel measure μ supported a.e. in `[a,b]`, `a ≤ b`, complex z outside that interval, mass `M=μ(R)` and `Fμ(t)=μ((-∞,t])`, the reconstructed identity is

`∫ 1/(z-x) dμ(x) = M/(z-b) − ∫_[a,b] Fμ(t)/(z-t)^2 dt`.

The sign follows from `d/dt (z-t)⁻¹=(z-t)⁻²`; finite mass, compact support and a nonzero denominator bound justify the interchange. Atoms are allowed. Endpoint convention differences are Lebesgue-null in the integration variable t, not μ-null in x. The point-mass case and mass-two mutation catch accidental probability normalization; a=b is a valid degenerate case (`sorrel.tex:954`, `:993`, `:1037`). The exact a.e. integral-congruence consumer need not require integrability; splitting an integral by subtraction is a distinct consumer that does (`:1061`). Failure of the outside-interval guard means that this route is unavailable, not that every possible integral statement is false; zero measure illustrates why. The Python Cauchy example is a symbolic dependency diagram, not a checked analytic theorem.

**Implementation sequencing.** Exact quotient with fixed data; two divisibility routes; measure-sensitive analysis transfer; then Leant/dependent constructions is a coherent implementation sequence (`sorrel.tex:1424`). A renderer-only outcome is explicitly acceptable. The finite model has a parser, alternative supports, conditional proof checker/exporter, policy-filter test, and budget flag. Lean expression grounding, metavariable freezing, real tactic calls, Cauchy compilation, and full policy compilation are not implemented (`:1465`, `:1534`).

## Common points, real differences, and new combined ideas

All three favor ordinary Lean acceptance first, same-object/structure-anchored properties, proof-producing bounded services, typed transport after rewrites, explicit realization for negative behavioral results, and matched evaluation with helper libraries and provider services available to the baseline. All separate source fidelity, method fidelity, and kernel validity. Agreement is largely a refined architecture and evaluation agenda; it is not independent experimental confirmation that a new language improves mathematics.

The strongest differences to preserve in the synthesis are: strict request sealing versus role-classified holes; one-proof closure with diagnostics versus all minimal conditional supports; known-fact-dependent saturation versus availability projection over a fixed support inventory; arbitrary realized-span coverage versus constructive semilinear image presentation; and route-preserving abstraction versus searching for a different route with fewer residual assumptions.

**New combined observation.** Rowan's finite test set consists of actual image bases and base-plus-generator points. Their lifted vectors span each supplied semilinear component. Laurel's criterion therefore provides a common interface for both: a witness family plus a checked coverage theorem. Rational linear algebra could compress a redundant semilinear test family to at most m+1 lifted vectors while preserving witnesses and coverage. This is a deduction from the two proposals, not an executed feature. It does not infer the image from samples or eliminate the source realizer obligation.

**Recommended API distinction.** Use separately typed results for `Diagnostic`, `ConditionalRoute(Δ, π)`, and `CompleteMinimalRoutes(profile, routes, completion evidence)`. A partial route remains sound without claiming final minimality. Preserve route-to-residual provenance after subtracting available assumptions, and distinguish an empty route family from a family containing the empty support. A single “open/supported” badge cannot carry all these meanings safely.
