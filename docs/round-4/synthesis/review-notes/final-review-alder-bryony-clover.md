## Material corrections

1. **Restore the intended Frey arithmetic helper; do not introduce a shared “full” Fermat-equation benchmark.** Draft `unified-report.tex:692`–`:713`, especially `:693`–`:703`, adds `a^p+b^p=c^p` and treats `(3,2,5)` as an inadequate full-context fixture. In Alder, Bryony, and Clover that triple is `(a,b,p)`, and the useful helper deliberately has **no Fermat equation**. Its assumptions are `2|b`, `4≤p`, odd p, and `a≡3 mod 4`. They are satisfied by `(a,b,p)=(3,2,5)` and give the exact quotients `q2=-53`, `q4=-486`. The proposed `(-1,0,-1,5)` does satisfy the newly written equation, so this is not a false arithmetic claim; it is a material change in attribution and benchmark scope, with a degenerate zero-b case replacing the meaningful original fixture. Replace the paragraph with the smaller helper. If some other proposal includes the Fermat equation, compare that separately as a different interface. Sources: `docs/round-4/ideas/Alder/Alder.tex:560`–`:607`; `docs/round-4/ideas/Bryony/Bryony.tex:369`–`:394`; `docs/round-4/ideas/Clover/Clover.tex:1527`–`:1579`. Also preserve Clover's useful refinement: the first coefficient's particular route only needs `p≥2` together with oddness and residue, whereas the second uses `p≥4` and no oddness/residue assumptions. The divisibility target itself remains the fundamental guard, not these sufficient hypotheses as a weakest contract.

2. **Negative realization needs negative transfer as well.** Draft `:535`–`:536` currently says that realizing a failing abstract observation means it really refutes the fixed candidate. Add the premise that the requested concrete specification would imply the abstract condition that failed. Positive soundness has the opposite direction and cannot supply this automatically. A concise replacement is: “Negative transfer and realization: the source specification implies the abstract condition at every admissible input, and the failing observation is realized by such an input.” Concretely, require `D(x) → Spec(c,x) → M(obs(x))`, then use `D(x0)`, `obs(x0)=u0`, and `¬M(u0)`. This follows directly from Bryony `:580`–`:589` and Clover `:1341`–`:1343`. Reflect the same distinction in the result table at draft `:1002`: a “model counterexample” can lack a source-transfer theorem even when an input realizer is known. The existing candidate-refutation row at `:1001` is already appropriately scoped to a proof of the source negation.

3. **Define total-support vocabulary before asserting the projection identity.** Draft `:406`–`:409` writes `Req_K(q)=Min{S\K : S∈S(q)}` without defining which assumptions total supports may contain. This equality is valid if `S(q)` is the inclusion-minimal supports over `K∪O` with no unaccounted background seeds (or with an explicitly equivalent fixed-background convention). If it is over all atoms U, filter to supports satisfying `S\K⊆O` before projecting. Otherwise the right-hand side can offer premises outside the permitted O. For example, with K empty, O={A}, and only rule `B→q`, a total support `{B}` over U is not an allowed explanation. Define `S(q)=Min{S⊆K∪O : q∈Cl_R(S)}` and the intended formula follows. The accompanying argument should retain the original total derivation, as the draft already correctly says.

4. **State smoothness for the general inverse-derivative formula.** Draft `:775`–`:786` says repeated differentiation locally where `f'≠0` yields the n-th formula; nonvanishing alone does not supply higher derivatives. Specify real `f` of class `C^{n+1}` on a suitable interval, a local inverse g, and nonvanishing f' there (or an explicitly corresponding local differentiability hypothesis for each displayed step), with `n≥1`. The recurrence itself is correct: differentiating `P_n/u1^(2n−1)` and multiplying by `dx/dy=1/u1` gives the denominator `u1^(2n+1)` and the displayed `P_{n+1}`. This is a missing hypothesis, not an algebraic error in the recurrence.

## Smaller precision improvements

- **Mandatory initialization:** draft `:317`–`:318` says each offered atom “may also” be initialized. For the completeness theorem that follows, every offered atom must be initialized with its singleton route, subject to domination by an already-known empty route. Replace “may also” with “is also,” or say the insertion may be immediately dominated. Otherwise the algorithm as literally described could omit an allowed proof-tree leaf.
- **Worst-case test count:** draft `:600`–`:605` is mathematically sound as a lower bound for certifying zero in the full affine hypothesis class. State “certifying that it is identically zero requires r+1 independent evaluations in the worst case.” A single nonzero evaluation already proves a form is not identically zero, so the current unrestricted “requires ... to determine whether” can be read too strongly.
- **Fully qualified semantic references, not only declarations:** draft `:853`–`:855` should recommend hygienic fresh/escaped declaration names **and fully qualified references to core predicates, composition, and theorem constants**. Namespace qualification of the newly emitted declarations alone does not prevent unqualified semantic references from resolving to a prior generated declaration.
- **Bryony attribution:** draft `:829`–`:830`, “treats named premises as authorities,” is unnecessarily ambiguous. The valuable actual distinction is exact full-snapshot certificate binding and the requirement to prove a premise before promotion. Suggested wording: “binds certificates to the full source snapshot and keeps offered premises conditional until proved.” Its opaque context/candidate/policy labels do not implement Lean context transport or policy filtering (`Bryony.tex:750`–`:755`).

## Valuable differences still worth retaining

1. **Publication policy is a design choice.** Draft already records Clover's incomplete document at `:850`–`:852`, but does not contrast it with Alder's all-or-nothing CLI. Alder refuses to create new output if any `show` fails; Clover exports accepted theorem nodes while marking the document incomplete. Both are legitimate. Add one sentence requiring distinct statuses for saved partial content, individually accepted assertions, and a completely verified document. This is more informative than treating partial output itself as a failure. Source: Alder `:1003`–`:1005`; Clover `:2086`–`:2091`.

2. **Quantified facts must remain schemes.** Draft's locality example `:658`–`:677` omits Clover/Alder's sharper implementation requirement: retain the regional induction hypothesis as `∀y∈U, P(y)`, rather than keeping only its already-used instance at x. The unspecialized fact supplies neighborhood equality and future instances. An actual future index should store binder telescopes and perform controlled specialization. Also preserve differentiability of G only on the attained image W(U), rather than silently strengthening to global differentiability. Source: Clover `:833`–`:882`, `:310`–`:342`; Alder `:504`–`:556`. Two sentences here would prevent this round's whole interface from appearing purely ground and propositional.

3. **Alder's formal-to-analytic upgrade deserves one concrete formula.** Draft `:768`–`:774` retains its lesson but loses most of its distinctive mathematics. The example `Δ+4Δ²=(4/9)Q` has zero-constant branch `(sqrt(1+(64/9)Q)−1)/8`; the other exact branch passes the residual check but fails the requested constant term. The independent analytic coefficient-ratio argument gives radius 9/64 and, for `|q|<9/64`, `N≥1`, tail at most `|a_Nq^N|/(1−64|q|/9)`. Adding the branch equation and explaining the other exact root would make the claim self-contained without reproducing the whole derivation. Source: Alder `:611`–`:720`. The finite companion checks neither a general analytic theorem nor an all-orders Lean formalization.

4. **Show Clover's actual profile numbers.** Draft `:844`–`:849` says the profile comparison is measured but never gives the result. One sentence—211 versus 16 generated instances and 633 versus 48 repeated-scan rule tests, both with a four-node retained proof—would substantiate the main engineering recommendation. These remain Python-model counts; no Lean speed comparison is implied. Source: Clover `:1967`–`:1988`, reproduced `profile-comparison.json`.

5. **Keep a positive a.e.-to-pointwise bridge available.** The draft rightly forbids unlicensed point evaluation. Alder and Clover also explain that continuity plus a measure positive on every nonempty open set permits upgrading a.e. equality to equality everywhere. Mentioning this avoids turning a conditional rule into an absolute ban. Source: Alder `:827`–`:844`; Clover `:916`–`:920`.
