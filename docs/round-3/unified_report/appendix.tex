\section{Report cards}\label{app:cards}
Each card records the report's thesis, the source files it opened beyond the round-2 set, what its Lean file contains and how it compiled, what its companion executed, and the weakness this review judges most consequential.

\subsection*{Basalt}
\textbf{Thesis.} ``This exact object has this type and these proved properties, in this scope''; unary refinements, behavioural contracts, and relational contracts on whole diagrams are three surface forms of one proved predicate. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{ContinuousCDF.lean} (\code{cdf_reflection_sub}, \code{measure_eq_withDensity_of_cdf_hasDerivAt}); FLT \code{Def_AdicCompletionRingFunctoriality.lean} and \code{S_Rep_indBotSC_shortExact.lean}; Leant \code{Length/Contract.hs} and \code{Length/Handoff.hs}. \textbf{Distinctive.} Exactness and diagram isomorphism as relational types with the $k\to k^3\to k$ and $\Z\xrightarrow{2}\Z\otimes\Z/2$ neighbours; ideal-preserving maps composed to quotient towers and completions; the Dirac atom correction to CDF reflection; a precision-demand transformer applied to jets and $I$-adic towers; realisability of abstract counterexamples stated with the $V_A=\{0\}$ argument; the parametricity caveat. \textbf{Lean.} \code{ContractCore.lean}, 74 lines, compiles; two theorems axiom-free; three \code{defProp} warnings. \textbf{Companion.} 24 test methods: sparse certificates, corrupted and mismatched inputs, a jet residual, the restricted length interpreter. \textbf{Weakness.} The three algebraic cases are the round's richest but their obligation structures are described, not encoded; the Lean file contains none of them.

\subsection*{Fiber}
\textbf{Thesis.} A refinement elaborator with an explicit carrier-preservation invariant, indices (locality, measure, precision, coefficient domain) as parameters of propositions, and behaviour flowing backward into synthesis holes. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{UniformSplineWeakConvergence.lean}; FLT \code{S_FreyPackage_freyCurveInt_map.lean}; Leant \code{docs/length-ranking.md}. \textbf{Distinctive.} The non-vacuous Frey benchmark $(\mathrm{F}_0)$ with inhabitant $(3,2,5)$ and three missing-premise neighbours; the centred coupling bound $\min\{2,|t|\delta_n\}$; same-carrier coherence as a proposition; backward constraints on holes with the completion/sketch/program distinction; a 24-row mutation register with a ``Finite'' column. \textbf{Lean.} \code{CoreEncoding.lean}, 67 lines, compiles; three theorems axiom-free; \code{certificate_bridge} takes soundness and execution as hypotheses, as the report says. \textbf{Companion.} 1,298 checks in 31 groups, including 288 Frey triples and 381 coupling instances, with dual-number evaluations to block field-only shortcuts. \textbf{Weakness.} The synthesis section describes Synquid-style decomposition for Lean propositions without saying which fragment the decomposition is complete for.

\subsection*{Gneiss}
\textbf{Thesis.} One operational discipline: a property view is an input to bidirectional checking, and a behavioural signature transfers views across a step; theorem application and verified computation implement the same signature. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{TriangularKernelInverse.lean} and \code{BinomialInversion.lean}; FLT \code{S_WeierstrassCurve_modularity_of_semistableModel.lean}. \textbf{Distinctive.} Prefix causality and the shift counterexample; the level-flow rules from squarefreeness; the binomial row counterexample; a dense univariate checker with denotation; \code{mvcgen} and Dijkstra monads as the effect axis; a 20-row suite with a status column. \textbf{Lean.} \code{GneissCore.lean}, 81 lines, compiles; two theorems axiom-free; four \code{defProp} warnings. \textbf{Companion.} 32 test methods modelling backward-only dependencies, relation kinds, precision loss, and the univariate checker. \textbf{Weakness.} The one-sided inverse theorem is stated for prefix-causal linear operators but the Mathlib lemma it needs is the deprecated matrix form; the general \code{IsDedekindFiniteMonoid} version is not mentioned.

\subsection*{Karst}
\textbf{Thesis.} Refine knowledge, not object identity: an anchored property view with scope-closed support, bidirectional checking with visible obligations, and behaviour typed at the right observational strength. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{NoFiniteModel/FiniteTypes.lean} and \code{Theorem.lean}; FLT \code{Def_FLTPrelim_FreyPackage.lean}, \code{S_FreyPackage_no_frey_package.lean}, \code{PROOF-PATH.md}; Leant \code{PostVerification.hs}. \textbf{Distinctive.} The finite injective-endomap induction as a transport workload; proposition-valued finiteness versus executable enumeration; the \code{List Empty} test; the exact-quotient interface; the five-arm evaluation $L_0$--$L_4$; an eighteen-row paired register. \textbf{Lean.} \code{KarstCore.lean}, 114 lines, compiles; eleven theorems axiom-free including \code{involutionInj}, \code{fixesPointPreservesComplement}, \code{surjectiveCannotOmit}, and \code{dropAllLengthOnEmpty}. \textbf{Companion.} 4,834 cases in eight groups: all endomaps of sizes zero to five, 720 denotation samples over $\Z$, $\Z/4$, $\Z/5$, 364 sorting samples, 312 exact-quotient samples. \textbf{Weakness.} Its Frey terminal sketch names \code{Mazur} and \code{Ribet} as aliases the report itself warns against; the aliasing rule is right but the sketch does not follow it.

\subsection*{Moraine}
\textbf{Thesis.} Evidence-indexed classification $A\triangleright\rho$ with anchors and telescopes; relational and scope-aware contracts; evidence-directed synthesis that separates carrier inhabitance, behavioural witness, and proof obligation. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{GKBProcessAux.lean}; FLT \codeb{S_Rep_nonempty_invariants_coind_equiv.lean} and \codeb{S_Algebra_exists_algHom_equiv_pi.lean}; Leant \code{BehavioralSelection/Internal.hs} and the host-native constraints report. \textbf{Distinctive.} Coinduced invariants as a bundle-adapter workload; naturality as a refinement of a family; the deletion budget $\mathrm{Close}_{kR}$; the complete affine coefficient criterion with its realisation clause; the candidate primitive refinement rules and a gate for trying them; the \code{fix}/\code{know}/\code{use} grammar. \textbf{Lean.} \code{Contracts.lean}, 194 lines, compiles; sixteen theorems, fourteen axiom-free, \code{model_sound} and \code{promote_model_spec} with \code{propext}. \textbf{Companion.} 32 tests including 25,600 interpreter-versus-model comparisons and 729 counterexample constructions. \textbf{Weakness.} It answers the twenty questions only implicitly and omits the round-2 CAS promotions, so its count of absent marks (12) overstates disagreement; its polynomial checker is absent by choice.

\subsection*{Obsidian}
\textbf{Thesis.} Property overlays at use sites, lawful-use signatures for operations, and witness-changing constructions as a third category beside refining and observing an unchanged object. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{MellinBose.lean}; FLT \code{S_FreyPackage_of_counterexample.lean} and \code{S_FreyPackage_freyCurveInt_map.lean}; Leant \code{Length/Handoff.hs} and \code{docs/synth-internals.md}. \textbf{Distinctive.} The vacuity warning for contracts tested under \code{FreyPackage}$\to$\code{False}; the $L^1$-summable family with the telescoping pulse counterexample; normalisation as a chain of new witnesses; the unexported exponent preservation; a read-only renderer arm $L_R$; a sparse checker with denotation and 32 symbolic difference-of-powers identities. \textbf{Lean.} No file; the appendix listing of \code{Observation.weaken} and \code{Observation.compose} is the same combinator the other files define. \textbf{Companion.} 608 probes: 396 admissible Frey triples including composite exponents, 32 symbolic identities, 41 negative neighbours, 9 protocol probes. \textbf{Weakness.} Without a Lean file it is the one report whose calculus was not checked here; its overlay rules are otherwise identical to the compiled ones.

\subsection*{Schist}
\textbf{Thesis.} Four layers (carrier and structure; proof-backed refinements; relations and observations; behavioural and operational contracts) sharing evidence handling but not a universal coercion; backward requirement transformers turn a chosen step into a typed residual. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{AffineIndependentCopy.lean}; FLT \code{Def_FLTPrelim_FreyPackage.lean} and the terminal contradiction; Leant \code{docs/length-ranking.md} and the Z3 behavioural proposal. \textbf{Distinctive.} The orbital-comparison theorem with its bounded-product refinement; \code{assume}/\code{derive}/\code{construct}; a lexical structure environment; Mizar soft typing and Isabelle locales as precedents; the only compiled checker of the round. \textbf{Lean.} \code{SchistCore.lean}, 140 lines, compiles; eight theorems, five axiom-free, \code{eval_nf}, \code{check_sound}, and \code{original_target} with \code{propext}; \code{corrupted_rejected} decided. \textbf{Companion.} 10 test methods with 650 affine point checks. \textbf{Weakness.} The affine checker's reification is definitional because the expression is built by hand; the report says so, but a reifier is the step that would make the slice real.

\subsection*{Tephra}
\textbf{Thesis.} A second, proof-relevant elaboration judgment above Lean's, with four strata (carrier, chosen structure, refinement, behavioural contract), object-bound structure selection, and a proof-linked registration layer over Leant's contracts. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{TriangularKernelInverse.lean}, \code{BinomialInversion.lean}; FLT \code{Def_FLTPrelim_GaloisRep.lean}; Leant \code{Fragment.hs}, \code{BehavioralSelection.hs}, \code{Length/Adapter.hs}, \code{Replay.hs}. \textbf{Distinctive.} The prefix-local inverse theorem via stable finiteness, with the halving-map neighbour; restriction of an action to an invariant subtype and the torsion instance; the image-of-length proposition and realisation contract; the parametricity caveat and uniformity contract; property-implicit binders; a five-cut ladder with exit conditions. \textbf{Lean.} \code{TephraCore.lean}, 119 lines, compiles; six theorems axiom-free including \code{restrict_comp} and \code{refute_universal}. \textbf{Companion.} 23 test methods with 100 valuations in $\Z$ and $\Z/4$ and prefix-sum inverse matrices for sizes one to eight. \textbf{Weakness.} Its answer to R10 is the most useful in the round but its own artifact is another full grammar, which its answer argues against.

\subsection*{Trellis}
\textbf{Thesis.} A property layer over ordinary dependent typing in which structure selection precedes property inference, behavioural contracts are first-class demands, and automation is finite and proof-explicit where it claims predictability. \textbf{New sources.} ProveIt \code{Regularity.lean}, \code{SP1Taylor.lean}, \code{SP2Parts.lean}; FLT \code{Def_FLTPrelim_FreyPackage.lean}; Leant \code{BehavioralSelection.hs}, \code{Length/Contract.hs}. \textbf{Distinctive.} The finite qualifier closure theorem; sharp Lipschitz constants with attainment; the guarded quotient derivative with its numerator identity separated from its guards; the vanishing-error theorem; gcd transport through power reflection; the rational affine-nonnegativity certificate; definitional functoriality and Lean4Lean as the kernel-research metatheory. \textbf{Lean.} No file. \textbf{Companion.} 31 tests (9 accept, 22 reject) over an exact-rational affine checker with origin digests and a prefix scope-weakening rule. \textbf{Weakness.} The closure theorem's rule generation, which decides whether the finite fragment is useful, is left to ``an earlier bounded step''.

\section{Experiments and reproducibility}\label{app:repro}
All Lean runs used the ProveIt checkout at commit \code{24ce8bd7} and its toolchain \codeb{leanprover/lean4:v4.32.0}, with the command \code{lake env lean <file>} from the ProveIt root on 6 September 2026. Files and logs are in \code{experiments/lean/}.

\begin{itemize}
\item \code{Basalt.lean}, \code{Gneiss.lean}, \code{Karst.lean}, \code{Schist.lean}: verbatim copies of the shipped files. \code{FiberAx.lean}, \code{MoraineAx.lean}, \code{TephraAx.lean}: verbatim copies with one \texttt{\#print axioms} line appended per theorem. Each \code{.log} is the complete compiler output.
\item \code{Supply3.lean}: sixteen \texttt{\#check} commands and four examples; \code{fun_prop} and the locality composite succeed, the deprecated matrix lemma warns, and the deliberate \code{mvcgen} misuse errors with the tactic's experimental warning, which is the evidence sought. Cold run 575 s.
\item \code{FreyExact.lean}: four theorems and six examples; compiles with the standard axioms. Warm run 220 s.
\item \code{tools/make_tables.py} regenerates \code{feature_matrix.csv}, \code{negative_suite.csv}, \code{question_tally.csv}, and \code{lean_cores.csv} and prints the counts used in Sections~\ref{sec:convergence}, \ref{sec:questions}, and \ref{sec:suite}.
\end{itemize}

The PDF is built by \code{build.sh}, which runs \code{latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error unified_report.tex}. The bibliography is embedded.
